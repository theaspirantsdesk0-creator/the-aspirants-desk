# Official UPSC previous-year-question archive

This directory tracks Civil Services Examination question-paper coverage and verification. It must remain separate from the topic-wise practice bank in `data/questions/`.

## Source of truth

- [UPSC Previous Year Question Papers](https://www.upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers)
- [UPSC Archived Question Papers](https://www.upsc.gov.in/archived-exam-documents/question-papers/)

## Status meanings

- `not_checked`: no source inspection recorded.
- `source_found`: a link to a document was found in the official UPSC index. This is discovery only; do not publish question text.
- `verified`: the official PDF itself was opened and its exam year, stage, subject and paper metadata were checked. Question text still needs its own transcription and editorial review.
- `published`: only use after the exact question and metadata have been checked against the PDF and the reuse approach has been reviewed.

A file-size record, search result, or official index link alone does not verify the contents of a PDF. A timeout or file-size limit is not evidence that a source is invalid; it means content verification remains incomplete.

## Answer and explanation policy

Do not label editorial answers as official UPSC answer keys. Add answers only where they can be supported independently; label them as editorial explanations. If no reliable answer can be established, leave it blank rather than guessing.

## Coverage target

The coverage manifest targets 2001–2025 for Prelims GS I and CSAT, Mains Essay and GS I–IV, all optional subjects and literature optionals. Targets are planning metadata, not a claim that those papers have been sourced, verified or published.

## Copyright and provenance

Prefer linking to official UPSC PDFs rather than reproducing full paper text. Preserve source URL, year, stage, paper and verification method for each record. Never convert discovery-only records into published question records automatically.
