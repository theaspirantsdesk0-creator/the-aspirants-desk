            State exam notifications

            Every state has its own public service commission and recruitment boards, and each posts notices on its own site. Open a state’s site, read the latest notices, then mark it checked. Use the live updates board to view notices published by the site administrator, and verify every notice on the commission’s official website. Add any date you find to the watchlist above.

            Web addresses come from a published list of commission sites. If one does not open, search the commission’s name, because states sometimes change their addresses. Recruitment boards such as police, teachers and subordinate services often post on separate sites, so look for a recruitment or notifications link on each state’s home page.

                  About me

                  I am a B.Tech Civil Engineering graduate from Puducherry Technological University, schooled in Chennai, and I speak Tamil, English and Telugu. Most of my learning has happened on construction sites, where a design meets budgets, deadlines and the people who will live in the finished building. That is why I am drawn to civil services: every project I worked on existed because of a scheme, a department and an officer who approved it.
