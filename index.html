              Exam directory

              A quick map of the main central and state government exams. Eligibility, age limits and vacancies change every year, so use this to choose, and the official notification to apply.

              State exam notifications

              Every state has its own public service commission and recruitment boards, and each posts notices on its own site. Open a state’s site, read the latest notices, then mark it checked. Use the live updates board to view notices published by the site administrator, and verify every notice on the commission’s official website. Add any date you find to the watchlist above.

              Web addresses come from a published list of commission sites. If one does not open, search the commission’s name, because states sometimes change their addresses. Recruitment boards such as police, teachers and subordinate services often post on separate sites, so look for a recruitment or notifications link on each state’s home page.

                    How The Aspirant’s Desk works

                    Follow these four steps every day. The whole site is built around them.

                    1. Plan

                    Open the Study planner, enter your exam date and daily hours, and get a weekly timetable.

                    2. Read

                    Read the Subject notes and the day’s PIB and press items.

                    3. Test

                    Tick what you read today and take the daily test the site generates from those notes.

                    4. Practise and track

                    Solve PYQs, log hours in My tracker, and revise weak topics on your revision day.

                    Study planner

                    Tell the site how much time you have. It builds a weekly timetable with a news slot, subject blocks, a daily test and a revision day.

                    Daily test

                    Tick the topics you read today. The site makes a quick test from those notes. These are practice questions made from the notes, not official UPSC questions.

                    Subject notes

                    Revision notes for each UPSC subject. Pick a subject. The owner can add more notes for any topic, and they appear here for everyone.

                    Daily press information

                    Daily news explained for the exam: what happened, the background, and the Prelims and Mains angle. Posted by the owner.

                      PIB current affairs

                      Releases from the Press Information Bureau, explained for the exam. Newest first. The owner posts new items daily, and the source link opens the official release list.
