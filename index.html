About The Aspirant’s Desk

An engineering-grade, distraction-free study operating system for self-learners preparing for UPSC Civil Services, State PSCs, SSC, and Banking examinations.

Cloud + Offline Hybrid

Every study session, test score, syllabus checkmark, and Mains draft saves locally for instant speed and synchronizes to Supabase when online.

Evidence-Based Recall

Combines active recall flashcards, negative-marking Prelims simulations, and 1-3-7-21-45 day spaced repetition.

Public-Spirited & Free

Zero advertisements, zero paywalls, and full DPDP Act 2023 data privacy compliance.

How The Aspirant’s Desk works

Follow these four steps every day. The whole site is built around them.

1. Plan

Open the Study planner, enter your exam date and daily hours, and get a weekly timetable.

2. Read

Read the Subject notes and the day’s PIB and press items.

3. Test

Tick what you read today and take the daily test the site generates from those notes.

4. Practise and track

Solve PYQs, log hours in My tracker, and revise weak topics on your revision day.
